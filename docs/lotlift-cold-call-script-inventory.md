# LotLift cold-call script inventory

**Source:** `/Users/imorgado/Downloads/Objections (1).pdf` (extracted 2026-09-08).

This is a review artifact, not a live-call authorization. Use only approved product facts,
pricing, lead-source claims, and calendar access. An explicit do-not-call request must end
contact and trigger the existing suppression process.

## Gatekeeper

### G1 — Identify the process owner
- **Trigger:** “What is this regarding?”
- **Required call context:** You are speaking to a gatekeeper; the process owner and configured rep name are available.
- **Exact rep sentence:** “It’s about how the dealership covers paid online inquiries from marketplace listings. Could you let [process owner name] know [configured rep name] from LotLift called?”
- **Follow-up sentence:** “Is [process owner name] the owner of that process, or is there an internet-sales or sales manager I should speak with?”
- **Objective:** Reach or correctly identify the owner, GM, sales manager, internet manager, or BDC manager.
- **Prohibited claims:** Do not say [process owner name] told you to call, that it is personal, that you are returning a call, or that this is not a sales call.
- **Appointment-booking exit condition:** The process owner agrees to a specific 15-minute workflow call; otherwise record the correct owner or exit.
- **Owner approval before live:** No.

### G2 — Verified marketplace-source context **[OWNER APPROVAL REQUIRED]**
- **Trigger:** Gatekeeper asks what the call concerns and a named marketplace source is known.
- **Required call context:** The named source is verified for this dealership from an approved, current source; the configured rep and process-owner names are available.
- **Exact rep sentence:** “It’s a quick question about the [verified marketplace source] inquiry workflow. Could you let [process owner name] know [configured rep name] from LotLift called?”
- **Follow-up sentence:** “Who owns that process there?”
- **Objective:** Make the handoff relevant without a feature dump.
- **Prohibited claims:** Do not name a source without verified provenance; do not imply access to customer leads or internal systems.
- **Appointment-booking exit condition:** The confirmed owner accepts a workflow call.
- **Owner approval before live:** Yes — approve the dealership/source-data provenance and permitted wording.

### G3 — Sales-call disclosure
- **Trigger:** “Is this a sales call?”
- **Required call context:** The caller is still determining ownership and fit; the owner-approved email-contact policy permits asking for a preferred address.
- **Exact rep sentence:** “It may lead to one if there is a real gap, but right now I’m trying to find out who owns the online-lead response process. Is that [process owner name] or someone else?”
- **Follow-up sentence:** “Is there a preferred email for whoever owns paid online inquiries?”
- **Objective:** Be direct and identify the correct contact.
- **Prohibited claims:** Do not deny the sales purpose or evade the question.
- **Appointment-booking exit condition:** Owner is identified and accepts a call; otherwise log the contact path and exit.
- **Owner approval before live:** Yes — approve the email-contact policy.

### G4 — Gatekeeper process refusal
- **Trigger:** “We do not take sales calls.”
- **Required call context:** Gatekeeper states a process refusal; no explicit opt-out was made, and the owner-approved email-contact policy permits asking for a preferred address.
- **Exact rep sentence:** “Understood. I don’t want to break your process. Is there a preferred email for whoever owns paid online inquiries?”
- **Follow-up sentence:** “So I send it to the right person, is [process owner name] the owner of that process, or is there an internet-sales or sales manager I should speak with?”
- **Objective:** Respect the process while identifying a permitted contact route.
- **Prohibited claims:** Do not continue if the refusal becomes an explicit opt-out; do not evade a sales-call question.
- **Appointment-booking exit condition:** Only continue if a permitted route is offered; otherwise end the call.
- **Owner approval before live:** Yes — approved email policy is required.

### G5 — Explicit do-not-call **[OWNER APPROVAL REQUIRED]**
- **Trigger:** “Take us off your list.”
- **Required call context:** An explicit do-not-call or opt-out request was made and the verified suppression control is available.
- **Exact rep sentence:** “Absolutely. I’ll mark this number do-not-call. Thanks for letting me know.”
- **Follow-up sentence:** None — end the call immediately.
- **Objective:** Honor the opt-out and trigger suppression.
- **Prohibited claims:** Do not ask for email, offer alternatives, schedule a callback, or make any further sales statement.
- **Appointment-booking exit condition:** None — immediately end the call and suppress future contact.
- **Owner approval before live:** Yes — live use requires verified do-not-call suppression.

## Decision-maker

### D1 — Current coverage check
- **Trigger:** “We’re not interested.”
- **Required call context:** Decision-maker has made a reflex/brush-off statement; no known fit conclusion.
- **Exact rep sentence:** “Fair enough. Most stores would not be looking for another tool. Before I close this out, is that because marketplace inquiries are already consistently covered there, or because that just is not a priority right now?”
- **Follow-up sentence:** “What would have to happen for lead-response coverage to become worth revisiting?”
- **Objective:** Distinguish strong coverage from low priority or disqualify.
- **Prohibited claims:** Do not argue, pitch more features, or treat a reflex answer as proof of a problem.
- **Appointment-booking exit condition:** Book only if a real coverage gap or future trigger is stated; otherwise close professionally.
- **Owner approval before live:** No.

### D2 — BDC/internal ownership check
- **Trigger:** “We have a BDC / internet department” or “Our salespeople handle it internally.”
- **Required call context:** The dealership says an internal team owns leads; owner-approved calendar availability and booking authority are available.
- **Exact rep sentence:** “That is usually the right setup. How does the team verify coverage when an inquiry comes in after hours or the usual person is off?”
- **Follow-up sentence:** “If it is the handoff or visibility piece that gets messy, would a 15-minute workflow check be useful?”
- **Objective:** Learn whether ownership and after-hours coverage have a real gap.
- **Prohibited claims:** Do not imply their team mishandles leads or promise replacement of the BDC.
- **Appointment-booking exit condition:** A stated handoff/visibility issue and agreement to review it; otherwise acknowledge they are covered.
- **Owner approval before live:** Yes — approved calendar availability and booking authority are required.

### D3 — Low-volume or unsupported-source disqualification
- **Trigger:** “We don’t get enough leads for this” or “We don’t use Cars.com / CarGurus / AutoTrader.”
- **Required call context:** Lead volume or supported inbound source is unknown.
- **Exact rep sentence:** “That may mean LotLift is not worth adding. Roughly how many paid online inquiries do you get in a normal month?”
- **Follow-up sentence:** “Which online sources generate most buyer inquiries, if any?”
- **Objective:** Disqualify low-volume or unsupported workflows quickly.
- **Prohibited claims:** Do not force a sale or claim an unsupported source works.
- **Appointment-booking exit condition:** Book only when an approved, meaningful source and a plausible coverage problem exist.
- **Owner approval before live:** No.

## Existing solution / CRM

### C1 — CRM workflow gap
- **Trigger:** “We already have a CRM.”
- **Required call context:** CRM is named or claimed; no verified workflow gap.
- **Exact rep sentence:** “That makes sense. I would expect you to. When an online inquiry arrives, does it land directly in that workflow and get owned immediately, or is there still an inbox and handoff before it is worked?”
- **Follow-up sentence:** “Sounds like you have it buttoned up. LotLift probably is not useful there.”
- **Objective:** Test for inbox, ownership, or after-hours gaps rather than replace a working CRM.
- **Prohibited claims:** Do not say their CRM is bad or promise CRM replacement/integration.
- **Appointment-booking exit condition:** Book only if a concrete handoff gap is admitted.
- **Owner approval before live:** No.

### C2 — CRM/DMS integration requirement **[OWNER APPROVAL REQUIRED]**
- **Trigger:** “Do you integrate with our CRM or DMS?”
- **Required call context:** Current product integration scope has been approved by the product owner.
- **Exact rep sentence:** “Today, LotLift is not a CRM or DMS integration. It works around approved inbox lead flows and appointment workflow. Is direct CRM/DMS integration a hard requirement for you, or are you trying to solve a specific handoff issue?”
- **Follow-up sentence:** “If it is a hard requirement, disqualify cleanly.”
- **Objective:** Separate a hard integration requirement from the underlying workflow problem.
- **Prohibited claims:** Do not imply an integration exists, is planned, or will work with every CRM/DMS.
- **Appointment-booking exit condition:** Book only for a supported inbox/workflow scope; otherwise disqualify.
- **Owner approval before live:** Yes — approve current integration and appointment-workflow facts.

### C3 — Competitor or prior-vendor experience
- **Trigger:** “We already use [competitor]” or “We tried something like this and it didn’t work.”
- **Required call context:** Existing tool or prior failure is acknowledged.
- **Exact rep sentence:** “Got it. What made you choose them, and what does that process handle well for you?”
- **Follow-up sentence:** “Is there anything the team still has to do manually around lead routing, after-hours response, or appointments?”
- **Objective:** Learn actual requirements and leave a no-gap account alone.
- **Prohibited claims:** Do not conceal limitations or claim LotLift differs without approved evidence.
- **Appointment-booking exit condition:** A meaningful manual or coverage gap worth mapping.
- **Owner approval before live:** No.

## Price

### P1 — Early price range **[OWNER APPROVAL REQUIRED]**
- **Trigger:** “How much does it cost?” before discovery.
- **Required call context:** A current owner-approved published range exists.
- **Exact rep sentence:** “The published range is [truthful range]. Whether it is worth that depends on whether there is a real coverage gap and whether your workflow is supported. How are online inquiries handled today?”
- **Follow-up sentence:** “To avoid quoting a setup that cannot work for your store, can I ask two quick questions: which lead sources feed the inbox, and who owns response after hours?”
- **Objective:** Give truthful pricing while qualifying support and workflow.
- **Prohibited claims:** Do not invent a range, custom quote, discount, ROI, or unsupported setup.
- **Appointment-booking exit condition:** Book when scope inputs are available and a 15-minute workflow review is accepted.
- **Owner approval before live:** Yes — approve price range, quoting authority, and escalation path.

### P2 — Expensive / unclear value
- **Trigger:** “It’s too expensive” or “No budget.”
- **Required call context:** A concern has been stated but not isolated.
- **Exact rep sentence:** “I can see why you would want to be careful about another tool. When you say expensive, is the issue the monthly number itself, the setup effort, comparison with another option, or that the return is not clear enough?”
- **Follow-up sentence:** “So the real concern is [repeat it]. If we could address that through [a supported scope, success measure, or plan], would anything else stop you from moving forward?”
- **Objective:** Isolate the real concern before discussing a supported scope.
- **Prohibited claims:** Do not discount before understanding the issue; do not guarantee ROI.
- **Appointment-booking exit condition:** They identify a solvable, supported concern and accept a scoped review.
- **Owner approval before live:** No.

### P3 — ROI guarantee
- **Trigger:** “What ROI can you guarantee?”
- **Required call context:** No validated operating baseline or approved guarantee exists; owner-approved operating outcomes are available for discussion.
- **Exact rep sentence:** “I cannot responsibly guarantee a number before we know your lead volume, current process, team follow-up, source mix, and approved workflow.”
- **Follow-up sentence:** “We can agree on measurable operating outcomes—eligible leads captured, response coverage, appointments—and evaluate the business effect together.”
- **Objective:** Replace a guarantee request with measurable operating outcomes.
- **Prohibited claims:** Do not guarantee revenue, ROI, appointments, or lead conversion.
- **Appointment-booking exit condition:** They agree to define measurable, approved scope and outcomes.
- **Owner approval before live:** Yes — approve any metrics, pilot scope, or outcome language.

## Spouse / partner

### S1 — Decision stakeholder
- **Trigger:** “I need to talk to my partner / owner.”
- **Required call context:** A stakeholder is explicitly named; their concerns are not yet known, and approved current workflow, pricing, security materials, and calendar access are available.
- **Exact rep sentence:** “Of course. What will they want to know before they are comfortable with this?”
- **Follow-up sentence:** “Would it make sense to include them in a 15-minute decision call so they can evaluate the workflow, price, security, and fit directly?”
- **Objective:** Learn decision criteria and include the stakeholder directly.
- **Prohibited claims:** Do not call this a contradiction, infer a blocker, or pressure the contact to decide alone.
- **Appointment-booking exit condition:** The stakeholder or a delegate accepts a specific 15-minute decision call.
- **Owner approval before live:** Yes — live booking requires approved calendar access and approved security/price materials.

## Timing

### T1 — Busy now
- **Trigger:** “I’m busy / I’m in a meeting.”
- **Required call context:** The person is unavailable now; no later time is agreed, and owner-approved calendar availability is available.
- **Exact rep sentence:** “That is exactly why I do not want to pitch you in the middle of it. Is there a calmer 15-minute window this week to map the workflow, or should I close this out for now?”
- **Follow-up sentence:** “Would [approved time option one] or [approved time option two] be less disruptive?”
- **Objective:** Offer a respectful binary: a specific slot or a clean exit.
- **Prohibited claims:** Do not continue pitching or claim urgency they did not state.
- **Appointment-booking exit condition:** A specific approved slot is confirmed; otherwise close out.
- **Owner approval before live:** Yes — requires approved availability and calendar-booking authority.

### T2 — Call later
- **Trigger:** “Call me later.”
- **Required call context:** No cause or date for a later conversation is known; owner-approved callback policy and calendar availability are available.
- **Exact rep sentence:** “Happy to. What is changing later that would make this a better conversation then?”
- **Follow-up sentence:** “Would [approved time option one] or [approved time option two] be less disruptive?”
- **Objective:** Identify a real event/date or avoid an indefinite chase.
- **Prohibited claims:** Do not treat an unspecified callback as consent for repeated contact.
- **Appointment-booking exit condition:** A concrete date/time or a stated event with a documented follow-up date.
- **Owner approval before live:** Yes — callback cadence and calendar access need owner approval.

### T3 — Next quarter / contract timing
- **Trigger:** “Call back next quarter” or “We’re locked into a contract.”
- **Required call context:** Renewal or triggering event is not yet logged; owner-approved callback and calendar policy is available.
- **Exact rep sentence:** “What happens next quarter that does not exist now—budget, contract renewal, staffing, or a new lead source?”
- **Follow-up sentence:** “Let’s put the actual date on the calendar if it is a real event.”
- **Objective:** Capture a concrete trigger and renewal date; otherwise close the loop.
- **Prohibited claims:** Do not fight a real contract or invent a renewal date.
- **Appointment-booking exit condition:** A real date/event is recorded and a permitted follow-up is scheduled.
- **Owner approval before live:** Yes — callback policy and calendar booking require approval.

## Send-info

### I1 — Requested information **[OWNER APPROVAL REQUIRED]**
- **Trigger:** “Send me information.”
- **Required call context:** Prospect has not specified the information needed; approved collateral, email route, follow-up permission, and calendar availability exist.
- **Exact rep sentence:** “Happy to. So I do not send generic software material, what are you most trying to understand: how it works with your current process, supported lead sources, security, or pricing?”
- **Follow-up sentence:** “After you look at that, would it make sense to spend 15 minutes at [approved follow-up time] and decide whether it is relevant?”
- **Objective:** Send only requested, approved material and propose a decision conversation.
- **Prohibited claims:** Do not send generic/unapproved collateral or imply support/security facts not approved.
- **Appointment-booking exit condition:** Prospect selects a topic and accepts a specific follow-up; otherwise send only approved requested material.
- **Owner approval before live:** Yes — collateral, outbound-email policy, and calendar follow-up must be approved.

### I2 — Proposal or quote request **[OWNER APPROVAL REQUIRED]**
- **Trigger:** “Just email the proposal / quote.”
- **Required call context:** Lead sources, ownership, approval requirements, and pricing authority are not fully qualified; owner-approved calendar availability and booking authority exist.
- **Exact rep sentence:** “I can do that. I do not want to send a proposal that ignores the lead sources, ownership, and approval requirements we just discussed.”
- **Follow-up sentence:** “A 15-minute review lets us decide whether there is even a workable scope. Can we do [approved time option one] or [approved time option two]?”
- **Objective:** Avoid an inaccurate proposal and establish whether a workable scope exists.
- **Prohibited claims:** Do not send an unapproved quote, proposal, custom scope, or pricing promise.
- **Appointment-booking exit condition:** A qualified review is booked; otherwise send only owner-approved published information.
- **Owner approval before live:** Yes — proposal/quote and scheduling authority are required.

## Not interested

### N1 — Second substantive no
- **Trigger:** A second substantive no after one clarification attempt.
- **Required call context:** The prospect has already declined and has not invited more discussion.
- **Exact rep sentence:** “Understood. I’ll leave it there. Thanks for your time.”
- **Follow-up sentence:** None — end the call immediately.
- **Objective:** Respect the refusal and end professionally.
- **Prohibited claims:** Do not ask another question, offer a checklist, manufacture urgency, or schedule a callback.
- **Appointment-booking exit condition:** None — immediately end the call.
- **Owner approval before live:** No.

### N2 — Happy with current process
- **Trigger:** “We’re happy with what we have.”
- **Required call context:** This is the first substantive refusal; no second no or opt-out was made.
- **Exact rep sentence:** “Good—if it is working, you should keep it. What are you using today to make sure paid online inquiries do not sit unworked?”
- **Follow-up sentence:** “Would it be useful to talk through after-hours and ownership, or are you comfortable leaving it as-is?”
- **Objective:** Learn one fact only if welcomed; otherwise exit gracefully.
- **Prohibited claims:** Do not badmouth their process, press after a second no, or manufacture urgency.
- **Appointment-booking exit condition:** Only book if they explicitly request a further workflow discussion; otherwise close the call.
- **Owner approval before live:** No.

## Callback

### B1 — Priority-based callback
- **Trigger:** “I’ll call you when things slow down.”
- **Required call context:** The stated priority and likely end date are unknown.
- **Exact rep sentence:** “Makes sense. What specifically is taking priority right now, and when does that ease up?”
- **Follow-up sentence:** “If there is no likely window, I will close the loop rather than keep chasing you.”
- **Objective:** Capture a real callback condition or end pursuit.
- **Prohibited claims:** Do not promise repeated outreach without a stated date or permission.
- **Appointment-booking exit condition:** A specific callback date/time is agreed; otherwise close the loop.
- **Owner approval before live:** Yes — approved callback policy and calendar authority required.

### B2 — Stakeholder callback
- **Trigger:** “I’ll show the demo to my owner / team.”
- **Required call context:** The stakeholder is known but absent; direct evaluation, owner-approved calendar availability and booking authority, and approved current materials are available.
- **Exact rep sentence:** “I appreciate that. The useful part is mapping their concerns directly—especially around cost, lead access, and workflow.”
- **Follow-up sentence:** “Could we include them for the last 15 minutes so they can evaluate it once rather than relying on you to relay it?”
- **Objective:** Include the actual decision stakeholders without pressure.
- **Prohibited claims:** Do not imply stakeholder approval, data-access permission, or a decision already exists.
- **Appointment-booking exit condition:** Stakeholders accept a specific decision call; otherwise record the requested callback path.
- **Owner approval before live:** Yes — booking authority and approved cost/access/workflow materials required.

### B3 — No-data first step
- **Trigger:** “We do not want to share lead data yet.”
- **Required call context:** Data access is not authorized; an owner-approved data-free workflow discussion and controlled-test policy are available.
- **Exact rep sentence:** “That is fine. The first step can be a workflow discussion using no customer data.”
- **Follow-up sentence:** “We should only move to a controlled test once access, authorization, and data handling are agreed.”
- **Objective:** Keep the first conversation data-free and preserve a permission gate.
- **Prohibited claims:** Do not request, access, or imply access to customer data before authorization.
- **Appointment-booking exit condition:** A workflow-only discussion is booked; any controlled test requires separate authorization.
- **Owner approval before live:** Yes — data handling, access, authorization, and controlled-test policy require owner approval.
